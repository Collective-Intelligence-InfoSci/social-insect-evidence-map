## Copy-ready description

This systematic review and evidence map examines behavioral specialization, worker inactivity, and performance in social insects. It distinguishes specialization–performance relationships, inactivity–performance relationships, and compensation after perturbation, separating behavioral recruitment from recovery of completed work. Laboratory and field evidence will be mapped by taxon, colony stage, design, measurement definition, observation window and outcome level. Quantitative synthesis will be conducted only for sufficiently comparable studies with usable effect estimates, uncertainty and defensible dependence handling. Cross-species raw-data reanalysis is outside the current protocol pending a separate rights and methods audit. Preparatory searching and preliminary assessments predate registration and will be disclosed. Greg Chism leads and administers the review; Daniel Charbonneau and Nitika Sharma coordinate synthesis and statistics. All contributors are affiliated with the College of Information Science, University of Arizona. The review has no dedicated funding and no declared conflicts of interest.
